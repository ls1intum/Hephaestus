# Artifact-source contract

Hephaestus may review work against a practice only from evidence with explicit semantics. Contract `1.0.0` defines the
logical source catalog, capture states, evidence profiles, and practice requirements. A capture manifest records
what was supplied to one run; it does not prove that a practice is empirically observable. Independent
validation of automated review has separate provenance and status.

> If any required evidence clause is unsatisfied, record insufficient evidence and make no practice claim.
> Missing evidence is never `NOT_APPLICABLE`, a strength, or evidence that desired behaviour is absent.

## Contract boundary

The contract separates three identities:

1. **Source catalog:** logical source kinds and their authority, timing, freshness, completeness, privacy, and
   absence-state semantics.
2. **Evidence profile:** the source allow-list for one type of reviewed work and runtime purpose.
3. **Capture manifest:** the state, provenance facts, and materialized artifacts for one invocation.

Paths are materializations, not source identities. Prompts, practice files, task envelopes, runner code, model
settings, and executables are invocation inputs and require separate evaluation provenance.

Runtime components must reference the exact contract version and catalog digest they implement. The
`schemaVersion: 1` context index is not an artifact-source manifest and must not acquire `1.0.0` semantics.
Inputs outside the catalog cannot satisfy a practice requirement.

This contract governs automated-review evidence, not every file used by the separate Mentor conversation
runtime. Mentor inputs remain subject to Mentor consent and integration controls and cannot support an automated
practice review. Reusing one for review requires registering it as a source kind first.

## Source vocabulary

### Authority and time

| Authority | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `UPSTREAM_SNAPSHOT` | Content captured from an upstream object. |
| `SYNCHRONIZED_MIRROR` | A local projection whose currentness depends on upstream synchronization. |
| `DETERMINISTIC_DERIVATION` | A deterministic transform of declared dependencies. |
| `LOSSY_DERIVATION` | A sample, excerpt, aggregation, heuristic, or other information-losing transform. |

Capture time is separately `EVENT_TIME_SNAPSHOT`, `COLLECTION_TIME_SNAPSHOT`, or
`PINNED_IMMUTABLE_IDENTITY`. An available capture records representation fidelity as `EXACT`,
`LOSSLESS_DERIVATION`, or `LOSSY_DERIVATION`.

A database row is not automatically authoritative for the current state of GitHub, GitLab, Slack, or Outline.
Currentness depends on a source watermark, event time, or immutable identity defined by the source contract.

### Privacy classes

Privacy class describes risk; it does not authorize collection, disclosure, or model egress.

| Class | Scope | Minimum treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PUBLIC` | Content verified as intentionally public within the approved scope | Integrity and purpose controls |
| `INTERNAL` | Non-public project or workspace content without person-level review findings | Workspace authorization and purpose-bound retention |
| `PERSONAL` | User-linked project content, authorship, reviews, comments, or activity | Personal-data access, export, and erasure coverage |
| `SENSITIVE_PERSONAL` | Private conversations or derived person-level review findings | Restricted audience and stricter disclosure and retention controls |

Each catalog source references separate source-use decisions whose purposes are machine-enforced. Capture requires
`AUTOMATED_PRACTICE_REVIEW`; finding persistence, delivery, and contributor evidence APIs require
`PRACTICE_FEEDBACK_DELIVERY`; mentor reuse requires `CONVERSATIONAL_MENTORING`; and workspace-admin evidence review
requires `OPERATOR_EVIDENCE_REVIEW`. Processor, region, transfer, and model-egress controls remain separate
deployment obligations. A source allowed in an internal mirror is not thereby allowed in an external model request.

## Source catalog (`1.0.0`)

The machine-readable [catalog](https://github.com/ls1intum/Hephaestus/blob/main/server/src/main/resources/contracts/artifact-source/1.0.0/catalog.json),
its adjacent JSON Schemas, and the cross-resource and identity constraints enforced by
[`check:contracts`](https://github.com/ls1intum/Hephaestus/blob/main/scripts/validate-artifact-source-contracts.mjs)
jointly define conformance. Schema validation alone is insufficient because standard JSON Schema `uniqueItems`
compares whole values rather than a selected identity property. Every consumer must also reject duplicate source,
artifact-path, practice, source-check, and reason-code identities; mismatched report/decision times; inconsistent
readiness outcomes; and catalog/profile/governance references that do not agree. Prose does not maintain a second
field-by-field catalog. Authoring tools resolve source kinds through one of the catalog's exact evidence profiles
and reject unknown kinds.

The constraints with direct authoring consequences are:

- Only sources pinned to an immutable upstream identity — the pull-request diff and the repository tree —
  expose a `CURRENT` claim. Every mirrored source, including the pull-request and issue records themselves,
  can be required only with freshness `NO_REQUIREMENT`.
- Outline documents cannot satisfy `COMPLETE`.
- Pull-request and issue core records cannot be logically empty. Other `1.0.0` sources may be validly empty.
- An available pull-request diff is pinned and complete; it may be empty but is never partial.

`ENGINEERING_BASELINE` with `ENGINEERING_APPROVED` records maintainer approval of the shipped, minimized product
scope. It is not controller or DPO approval and cannot cover an expanded source, purpose, audience, processor,
retention policy, or collection scope. `CONTROLLER_DECISION` records the releasable subset of a traceable decision;
only an unexpired `APPROVED` decision passes when that basis is used. The controller's complete record remains
separate. The reviewed decision is the gate that runtime configuration cannot waive: a source whose decision is missing,
refused, or expired is never read, whatever the deployment sets. Within that boundary, connecting a workspace
integration and enabling a practice is what authorizes the everyday code sources the product exists to read, so a
default deployment reviews pull requests and issues without further configuration.

One operator control sits on top: `hephaestus.evidence.sensitive-source-uses` grants explicit `source:purpose`
pairs for `SENSITIVE_PERSONAL` sources, which are read only after a deliberate decision. Wildcards are rejected,
and listing a non-sensitive source is a startup error rather than a silent no-op, so the grant list cannot quietly
grow back into an everything-list. Withholding an everyday source needs no separate switch: a practice author
chooses which sources a practice uses, and an integration that is not connected yields nothing.

Requiring operators to re-declare the sources the product exists to read would minimise no data — the deployment
and the workspace connection already state that purpose — while a prompt that fires on every source trains
operators to wave through the sources where a deliberate answer actually carries information.

Changing a source's meaning, selection scope, limit, mapping, freshness policy, dependency, or normalization
requires a new contract version. Repository history is not part of `scm.repository.tree` and requires its own
source and governance decision.

## Capture state

Source absence is separate from quality so the manifest does not collapse unavailable, stale, and partial evidence into one
state.

### Availability

- `AVAILABLE`: capture facts and logical content state are present.
- `NOT_COLLECTED`: the source was outside the plan, disabled, or not attempted.
- `UNAVAILABLE`: the source could not be retrieved for a non-error reason.
- `REDACTED`: the entire source was withheld for privacy or security.
- `COLLECTION_ERROR`: an attempted collection failed.

A non-available source contains no artifacts or source identifiers. Safe, typed reason codes may be
role-filtered; free-form exception text must not enter the manifest.

### Available-source dimensions

Only `AVAILABLE` has:

- logical content: `EMPTY` or `NON_EMPTY`;
- completeness: `COMPLETE`, `PARTIAL`, or `UNKNOWN`;
- capture and optional source times or immutable identity;
- query scope, completeness basis, fidelity, and artifacts.

Logical content is independent of serialized bytes: a JSON file containing `[]` represents an `EMPTY` source.
Field-level redaction remains an available, usually partial source; whole-source redaction uses `REDACTED`.

Freshness is a readiness result rather than a mutable capture fact, and only demonstrated staleness skips
automated review. `UNKNOWN` records that the runtime cannot establish currentness, which is not evidence that the
copy is out of date. `NOT_APPLICABLE` records that the contract offers no currentness claim for the source at all,
so authoring validation rejects a `CURRENT` requirement on it; `NO_REQUIREMENT` accepts the absence of a claim
without asserting that the data is current.

There is deliberately no age-based freshness mode. An age threshold requires a watermark recording when a copy was
last *verified* against upstream. The mirror records only when a row was last *written*, and rows are written only
when the upstream record changed, so an age derived from it measures time since the last upstream modification.
Applied as a freshness test, that reports a correctly mirrored record which has not changed recently as stale, and
a record modified moments ago as current. The five-minute window used previously therefore skipped automated
review for every backfilled review, every replay, and every review of an established repository, without
identifying a single case of genuinely out-of-date evidence.

Adding an age-based mode has a prerequisite: the synchronization layer must record a verified-as-of watermark,
advanced by a reconciliation pass that confirms a record is unchanged. Such a pass is the equivalent of an HTTP
`304`, which [RFC 9111 §4.3.4](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9111.html) defines as refreshing a stored
response without altering it. Until that watermark exists, a currentness guarantee on a mirrored source would be a
claim the system cannot substantiate.

Review readiness is a pure function of the manifest, the practices, the temporal anchor, and the source contract,
which is what makes a replay reproducible. A replay supplies the recorded anchor; supplying the current instant
would evaluate the evidence against a different moment and can change a result that was correct when it was first
produced. A manifest captured under a contract version the runtime no longer ships is *unreplayable* rather than
invalid: the recorded result stands, and only the ability to re-derive it is unavailable.

Deliberate ablation is an evaluation transformation, not natural absence. Production rejects every transformed
view. The manifest can record evaluation transformations, but this contract version does not apply or authorize
them; an evaluation runtime must define and validate its own dependency-aware view before enabling ablation.

## Practice automated-review policy

The [practice review glossary](./practice-review-glossary.mdx) defines the canonical product and machine
terms. Evidence requirements pin one source-contract version and evidence profile, then list required evidence and
optional context. Required sources must satisfy their completeness and freshness requirements. Optional context never controls review readiness and has no completeness or freshness fields.

The contract deliberately has no arbitrary expression language and cannot require `NON_EMPTY`. A successful query
that returns no items is valid evidence when the source contract allows empty content.

```json
{
  "sourceContractVersion": "1.0.0",
  "evidenceProfile": "pull-request-review",
  "automatedReview": {
    "mode": "LANGUAGE_MODEL",
    "evidenceSufficiency": "SUFFICIENT_WHEN_REQUIREMENTS_MET"
  },
  "requiredEvidence": [
    {
      "sourceKind": "scm.pull-request.diff",
      "completeness": "COMPLETE",
      "freshness": "CURRENT"
    }
  ],
  "optionalContext": [],
  "whenEvidenceIsInsufficient": "SKIP_AUTOMATED_REVIEW",
  "knownLimitations": [
    {
      "code": "RUNTIME_BEHAVIOR_NOT_OBSERVED",
      "description": "Runtime behaviour and production configuration are not observed."
    }
  ]
}
```

Validation rejects unknown versions or source kinds, sources outside the evidence profile, impossible quality
requirements, incompatible reviewed-work types, incoherent review settings, duplicate sources, and a source
listed as both required evidence and optional context.

`automatedReview` is explicitly relative to Hephaestus, the pinned source contract, the evidence profile, and
the governed integrations. It does not claim that a practice is universally observable. A developer, peer, or human mentor may have different context for a particular practice. This contract makes no claim that human review is always possible.

`mode` is `LANGUAGE_MODEL` or `NONE`. `evidenceSufficiency` says whether passing every requirement is
enough, the declared sources cannot support review, or automated review is not configured. These are author-defined
settings, not empirical validation. `automatedReviewValidation` is bound to the exact automated-review
policy and review-rule fingerprint. A reviewed status must also identify the exact evaluator procedure: model,
prompt, tools, and preprocessing. This release reports `AUTHOR_DECLARED`; independent validation is introduced by
[#1437](https://github.com/ls1intum/Hephaestus/issues/1437) and must record its evaluation protocol and scope.
Validation never certifies every future finding.

The current runtime admits only `LANGUAGE_MODEL` with `SUFFICIENT_WHEN_REQUIREMENTS_MET`. It skips:

- `DECLARED_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` with `DECLARED_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT`;
- `NONE` with `NO_AUTOMATED_REVIEW`.

A skipped practice is omitted from that review request; whether the workspace uses it in new reviews is
unchanged. Source readiness checks contain only
source-quality reasons such as `SOURCE_NOT_AVAILABLE`, `SOURCE_INCOMPLETE`, or `SOURCE_NOT_CURRENT`; review-mode
limitations are never misreported as defective sources. Missing evidence never becomes a fabricated
`NOT_APPLICABLE` finding.

Human review is outside this contract. A future self-review, peer-review, or human-mentor channel needs its
own review target, reviewer relationship, rubric, evidence provenance, consent, access, retention, result
vocabulary, and validation status. Merely declaring that a person could observe a practice neither collects a human
review nor makes an automated review ready to run. If a human review later becomes automated-review
input, it needs a governed source kind and purpose authorization.

## Conservative examples

### Required evidence is insufficient

`scope-one-reviewable-change` requires current, complete `scm.pull-request.core` and
`scm.pull-request.diff`. If the diff is unavailable or the pull-request watermark is stale or unknown, the
runtime records insufficient evidence and makes no claim that the change is focused, sprawling, or
`NOT_APPLICABLE`.

### Empty evidence is valid

A complete pull-request comment query may materialize as `[]`. Its state is
`AVAILABLE + EMPTY + COMPLETE`, not `UNAVAILABLE`. A capped/partial, redacted, or failed query cannot support the
same practice claim.

## Runtime behavior

The runtime resolves exact practice revisions before capture and collects the union of their governed source
requirements. Readiness is evaluated per practice. Optional-context failure does not skip an otherwise eligible
practice; required-source failure does. If no practice is eligible, no model request is made.

One invocation may contain the governed union needed by several practices. The model can technically read that
union; this runtime uses an attribution boundary rather than process isolation. The practice index lists each
practice's admissible logical source kinds. Every model-authored finding must cite an exact quote and artifact path.
The sandbox and server independently verify that the source is declared and available, the path belongs to that
source in the pinned manifest, and the quote occurs in the digest-addressed artifact. Deterministic secret-scanner
findings are the deliberate exception: the transient secret quote is verified by digest, then only its redacted
location is persisted. Delivery fails atomically on any mismatch.
This proves traceable grounding, not model causality; evaluations requiring non-interference must still run
practices in isolated evidence views.

The internal readiness report records whether each practice is ready or skipped. Model-visible input exposes only the evidence
needed for the review; governance metadata and protected reason codes remain internal.

## Change and staleness

Historical records keep their original contract and practice revision. Currentness is derived:

- source and profile resources are immutable within a published contract version; any semantic change creates a new
  version;
- moving a practice to that version changes its evidence requirements and stales that practice's earlier claims;
- changing a practice automated-review policy stales that practice's claims;
- adding a source cannot silently alter existing requirements because its exact version and source list remain
  pinned;
- governance withdrawal disables its exact future use, including collection, finding persistence and delivery,
  mentoring reuse, or evidence disclosure, without rewriting historical semantics; and
- erasure or expiry may make a claim unreplayable without making the historical result false.

Semantic currentness, automated-review-validation status, governance eligibility, and replayability remain separate.
Observation and review APIs expose `CURRENT`, `STALE`, or `UNVERIFIABLE` by comparing the evaluated practice
revision's review-rule fingerprint with the current revision's fingerprint. Presentation-only edits retain semantic
currentness; rows without comparable versioned fingerprints are `UNVERIFIABLE`.

## Adding or changing a source

Complete the [artifact-source governance decision](../admin/dsms/artifact-source-governance) before enabling a new
or expanded source use. Accuracy or product benefit alone does not authorize collection.

Tests must cover the runtime source inventory, every supported capture state, valid empty evidence, impossible
requirements, atomic capture, tenant isolation, retention and erasure, and safe telemetry.
