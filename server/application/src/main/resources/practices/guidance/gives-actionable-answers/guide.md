## How to do it

- Read the question again before you reply.
- Answer what the person asked, not a nearby question that is easier.
- When you know the answer, give it in the reply. A short answer is enough if the person can act on it.
- When the answer is in a document, name the exact page or section and the part that answers the question. Instead of “See the docs”, write “Set `retries` in the deploy config. The Retries section of the deploy guide has an example.”
- When you do not know the answer, name the next step. Say who to ask, or where they can look next.

## When it does not apply

- Your message does not answer a question.
- No one asked a question.
- Your reply is a reasonable partial step in a conversation that is still going on.
- The conversation has not settled yet, so there is no finished exchange to read.

## Common mistakes

- Writing a long reply so that it looks helpful. Length is not what helps. A short reply that gives the answer or the exact step is enough.
- Pointing at “the docs” or a whole wiki. Instead, name the exact section and the part that answers the question.
- Saying the problem back to the person. “That build fails a lot” tells them nothing new. Instead, add one thing they can do about it now.
- Answering a different question from the one they asked. Instead, answer their question first. Then add the other point if it still helps.
