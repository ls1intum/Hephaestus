A practice review can now say it could not tell. When the reviewer read the evidence a practice needs and that evidence genuinely did not settle the question, the observation is recorded as "Could not be determined" instead of being filed as "Not applicable". Those mean opposite things about a piece of work — "not applicable" says there was nothing here to look at — and until now the second was being written into developers' records in place of the first.
