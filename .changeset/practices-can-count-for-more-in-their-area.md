A practice can now count for more or less than its peers when its area is summarised. The weight applies to both halves of the area view — the standing and the direction it is heading — so an area led by the practices you care most about reads as those practices do. A weight of zero withdraws a practice from its area's summary entirely while leaving it fully reviewed, with its own card, standing and feedback unchanged: not counting toward the area is a different thing from not being looked at. Every practice starts at the neutral weight, so nothing changes until you set one.
