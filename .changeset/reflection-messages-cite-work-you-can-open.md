Messages on your reflection page now refer to your work by the number, title and link you would
recognise, so a reference you follow lands on the change it is about. They previously cited an internal
storage number, which read as a merge request or issue number and pointed at unrelated work — a message
about `!22` could arrive naming `#306`. Work that cannot be named — a chat thread, say — is now
described rather than numbered.
