The **Practice reviews** overview counts everything over one time range. It shows what needs an admin, the queue, what the reviews did, where their feedback went and how each practice was judged.

- The time range heads the page: **Today**, **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **All time**.
- **All time** starts at the first review of the workspace and is compared with nothing.
- A link from before that asked for 12 months opens **All time**.
- The overview keeps the chosen range while the browser tab is open.
- **Needs you** holds the feedback awaiting approval and the way to review it.
- **Queue** is one card. Under **Now**, it shows the reviews that wait, run and are on hold for budget.
- **Now** also shows the oldest wait, and the first reviews in line as a small table: work, developer, state and time.
- Under the name of the range, **Queue** shows the median time of whole reviews.
- One bar splits that time into **Until asked**, **Queue**, **Preparation**, **Execution** and **To developer**.
- Reviews that did not record when their execution started add a **Run (not split)** part. The trace of a review splits its run in the same way.
- **Finished**, **Lost to merge** and **Finished after merge** follow the review time.
- The (i) of **Finished after merge** says how many of those reviews the merge started.
- **What the reviews did** has the **Reviews** and **Observations** tiles. Their rows match, so their charts line up.
- The **Reviews** tile counts the reviews not finished yet and the share that failed or timed out.
- **Delivery** has the **Feedback** tile and **By channel and reason**.
- **By channel and reason** splits delivered feedback by channel and withheld feedback by reason.
- These tables use the words of the **Feedback** filters.
- Their foot gives the share of the feedback sent that failed to deliver.
- It also gives the share of the completed reviews whose results could not be processed or delivered. Failed and timed-out reviews count only on the **Reviews** tile.
- Every count on the tiles and on the Delivery card opens its list, filtered to exactly the rows it counts.
- **Waiting** opens the new **Waiting** status of the **Reviews** list. This status leaves out the reviews on hold.
- The **Practices** table shows each outcome as a part of the observations of the practice, for example "19 of 89".
- **Feedback delivered** is a part of the feedback composed about the practice. The table ends with an **All practices** row with the figures of the tiles.
- A line under the table says which practices the filters hide.
- The same line says why **Feedback delivered** can add up to a different number.
- **Needs a look first** puts first the practices marked **Never applies here** or **Often undecided**.
- **Busiest first** is still available. The chosen sort stays in the address.
- Numbers are black and labels grey, in one type scale.
- One colour rule: red means broken and fixable now, which applies to feedback that failed to deliver above 5 % and to reviews on hold for budget. The overview does not use amber.
- The (i) of the review time and of **By channel and reason** give one short line for each term.
- The overview reads the queue again only while a review waits or runs. A budget hold alone does not count.
- No list polls while the browser tab is hidden.
