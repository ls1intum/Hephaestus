Practice evidence is simpler to author and harder to get wrong. Choosing which sources a practice reads is now a single choice per source — required, optional context, or not used — and how completely a source must be captured is fixed by the source itself rather than restated on every practice that reads it. The named evidence profiles are gone; the sources available to a practice follow from the kind of work it reviews.

Two behaviour changes come with it: a review of a chat thread now waits for the whole thread rather than judging a fragment, and a practice that reads review comments is skipped when the comments could not be collected instead of being reviewed as though there were none. Both keep a collection problem from being recorded as something a developer did.
