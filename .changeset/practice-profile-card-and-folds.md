The Practice profile gets an easier feedback card, a label for open feedback and folds for what has nothing to report. It also gets a switch to Practices across the workspace.

- Each feedback card has four parts.
- The top says what the feedback is about. Its explanation is under **Why this was raised**.
- **Next step** says what to do.
- **Your last reviewed work** says how the feedback resolves.
- A line shows your reviewed work from oldest to newest, at most the five newest pieces.
- A wrench marks work that needed attention, and a check marks clean work.
- The clean run is joined in green. Empty dots show the clean pieces still missing.
- A thin mark with the caption **Feedback created** separates the evidence from the work after it.
- Clean work before the feedback has a grey check. Only clean work after the feedback counts.
- Beside the line are the count of clean work and your own answers under **Or mark it yourself**.
- The answers are **I fixed this** and **Does not fit my work**. A tooltip says what each answer does.
- The ratings are small icon buttons at the bottom right.
- A press anywhere on the card opens the practice.
- A withdrawn card keeps its short notice.
- **Has open feedback** shows beside each practice with open feedback in its group's table.
- One piece of work is enough for **Has open feedback**.
- Practices and groups that never applied to your work fold under **Does not apply to your work yet**.
- Practices that no review has observed yet fold under **Practices not observed yet**.
- Groups that no review has observed yet fold under **Practice groups not observed yet**.
- A group that mixes practices that never applied with practices not observed yet also folds there.
- A group with no practice to review folds there too.
- Each fold opens into rows of the table above it. Each row opens as any row does.
- A practice that never applied says so on its own level.
- The foot of **All practice groups**, a group and a practice links to Practices across the workspace.
- The links are **See all groups across the workspace** and **See this group across the workspace**.
- A practice has **See this practice across the workspace**.
- **See your own standing** leads back.
- The practice you came from is marked in its group, also for a screen reader.
- Switching back and forth adds one step to Back, however often you switch.
- The sentences behind a standing badge and a trend open in the same panel as the (i) buttons.
- Hover, keyboard focus and a tap open the panel. Escape closes it.
- A tap on a badge in a table row opens the sentence, not the row.
