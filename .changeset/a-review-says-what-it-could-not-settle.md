When a review cannot settle a practice, it now says what it could not settle and what would have
decided it. Previously "could not tell" was recorded as a bare verdict with nothing attached, which
told a reader neither what was in doubt nor how to resolve it. A review that stops short now names
the open question and the evidence it was missing — "the body of the issue the description defers
to" — so the gap is something you can act on rather than a shrug.
