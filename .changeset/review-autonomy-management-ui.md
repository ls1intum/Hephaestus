Adds **How much** under Administration → Practices → Review: one screen for deciding how much Hephaestus does on its own.

Set it once for the whole workspace and every area and practice follows, or make an exception for a single area or practice and hand it back later. The three steps — off, propose, deliver — read as one ladder, and a line that stays on screen tells you what the workspace is doing right now ("84 propose · 12 off · 4 deliver") without scrolling a hundred rows. Practices are grouped by area with each area's own counts, a filter narrows the list to just what somebody set by hand, and a selection can be moved in one action.

A second workspace setting decides where feedback may go at all: the mentor conversation only, or on the work itself as well. It can narrow where a practice speaks, but never give a practice more autonomy than its own tier allows.

Practices left alone keep behaving exactly as before.
