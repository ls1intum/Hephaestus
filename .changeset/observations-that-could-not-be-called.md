A practice review can now say it looked and could not tell. Reviews used to have only one way to be quiet — "this practice does not apply here" — so a check that read the evidence and genuinely could not decide was recorded as if the work had nothing to judge. Those two now read differently everywhere they are counted, so a quiet review no longer hides an undecided one, and a developer's history stops accumulating "nothing to see here" on changes where there was something to see.

Every observation also records what occasioned it — a review triggered by the work itself, or one somebody asked for by hand. Trends, summaries and the mentor's view read only the populations that are comparable, which keeps a bulk re-review of old work from showing up later as a change in how someone works.
