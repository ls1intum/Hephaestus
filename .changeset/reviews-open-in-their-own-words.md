A review comment now opens with a sentence written about your change. It used to open with one of a small set of fixed lines — "Nice work …", "Worth keeping: …" — so every review read the same way regardless of what was in it, and a change with a serious problem in it could still open on praise. The opening is now the review's own words, or nothing at all when it has nothing worth saying.
