What a practice review records about your work is now called an observation instead of a result, in the web app, the Chrome extension and the docs. For example, practice reviews have an **Observations** tab, the extension lists **Your observations**, and an outdated one is marked **Historical observation**. When Hephaestus refuses what a review proposed, it says the candidate observations were withheld.
