Practices that look for a problem can now say you avoided it. Eight practices — duplication, oversized
functions, swallowed errors, unvalidated input, unsafe crashes, insecure defaults and the rest — look for
something going wrong, and until now a change that did the work cleanly was recorded as "nothing here to
look at", the same value used when a change had nothing to do with the practice at all. A clean result on
work that genuinely engaged the practice is now recorded as a strength, so doing it right shows up on your
own practice pages instead of reading as though nobody looked.

This only applies where the practice states the boundary of what it searched — you cannot prove nothing
went wrong across code you did not read. Where it cannot state that boundary, the review says it could not
tell, as before.
