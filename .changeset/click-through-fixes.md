Practice reviews and the Practice profile read better after a walk through every screen:

- What the provider reported about a pull request or issue answers empty, not "not found", for work in a repository the workspace does not monitor. The work sheet asks once and says why its details are missing.
- An address of a review that does not exist answers at once, without retries, in one sentence.
- A practice pages card that is not opened yet says it is delivered when the developer opens it, not that comments are tried again.
- A reviewed revision shows the commit itself, without its `sha~` marker.
- Rows of work without a review name the repository once.
- Avatar initials come from words that start with a letter, so "Synthetic developer 05" reads "SD".
- The date picker ends today.
- The overview's Practices table keeps its Group filter and the turned-off switch in the address.
- The Practice profile's own link carries no default sort or tab.
- The Observations table shows the observation; what became of it is the trace's to tell.
- On a phone, filter chips sit side by side, and the practice tables in the Practice profile stack with the name first.
