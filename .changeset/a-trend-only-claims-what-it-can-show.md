A practice's direction now only ever says that something is moving, never that nothing is. "Broadly consistent lately" is gone: showing it needs far more reviewed work than a practice normally accumulates, and the honest reading of eight work items is that no direction is visible yet — which is what "Direction unclear" already says. The early-indication marker beside a direction is gone too; it labelled a difference that never changed which direction was shown.

A direction now appears once eight work items have exercised the practice — four to compare against the four before them — instead of seven with one side thinner than the other.

And a single setback no longer reads as though a habit had slipped. One problem on your newest work item moves a practice from doing well to mixed; it takes two in a row to read as needing attention, mirroring the two clean items it already takes to earn a strength back.
