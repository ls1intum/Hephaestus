Small fixes to the Practice profile and Practice reviews.

- A practice group whose every practice does not apply to your work says **Does not apply to your work yet** on its own level too, without a trend. A group in the fold of groups not observed yet says **Not observed yet**.
- The **Does not apply to your work yet** badge on a practice explains itself on hover, focus and tap, and the practice shows no trend.
- A workspace whose practice groups have no practices says **No practices set up yet** on **All practice groups**.
- A mouse click on a standing or a trend in a level's header opens its explanation.
- A feedback card counts every place its timeline leaves out in **earlier**, also evidence on work that later came back clean.
- While the Practice profile loads, no feedback card shows under the loading placeholders.
- A group's summary starts with a capital letter: "Five practices in this group".
- The trace says "merged after the review finished" when the work merged after the review finished but before its comments were ready.
- **See all** under the queue opens exactly the reviews it counts: waiting, running and on hold.
- The trace legend explains every delivery state, also **Rejected**.
- A trace filtered to one practice keeps only the moments that started or joined the review, as the other filters do.
- A long work label truncates beside its icon instead of wrapping under it.
- A long (i) panel scrolls inside itself on a short screen.
- Date range filters show the day before the month: "1 Sep – 10 Sep 2026".
- On a phone, the **Practices** table on the overview has one (i) under it that says how every column is counted.
