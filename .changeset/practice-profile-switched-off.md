A practice switched off for the workspace leaves the Practice profile, even where you held a standing on it: its standing, its feedback cards and its changes no longer show. A practice group with no practices, or with all of them switched off, folds under **Practice groups not observed yet**. A link to such a group opens a level that says the group has no practices instead of an empty table. Practices across the workspace counts the same way and leaves out a group the workspace reviews no practice in. Practice setup and the admin views still show every group, and the practice's observations and feedback.
