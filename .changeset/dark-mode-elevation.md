Dark mode separates its layers more clearly:

- A slide-in panel is lighter than the page behind it, and the page behind is dimmed more, so the panel stands out instead of blending into it.
- When a second panel opens over the first, the covered one turns darker, so the panel in front is easy to tell apart.
- Cards are slightly lighter than the page, and borders around cards, tables and panels are easier to see.

Light mode looks the same as before.
