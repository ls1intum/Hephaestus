Every detail panel now slides in as far as the panels of the Practice profile. A panel over another covers it to the same depth and leaves the same strip of it in view. This applies to Practice reviews, the trace, Practices across the workspace, activity, the catalog, surveys and Practice setup. The sheets of Practice reviews keep their text sharp once they have slid in.

The sheet of a piece of feedback gives its delivery outcome once. Its history then tells what became of each delivery, such as **Stopped trying**, with the attempts and the last error. A delivered piece of feedback no longer lists the same delivery twice.

The audit and Slack channel sheets dim the page behind them and no longer blur it. Ids in a sheet's technical facts fit on one line. The Reviews and Review panels of the Practice profile line up their title with the content like the other panels.
