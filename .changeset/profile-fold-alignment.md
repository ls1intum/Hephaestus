On the Practice profile, the folded practice groups and practices are part of the table above them: they share its columns on every screen width, with no second header or frame. A folded row shows its standing badge alone. The fold opens from a plain row with a chevron, and the folds of groups and practices without observations are called "Practice groups not observed yet" and "Practices not observed yet", like the standing they show.
