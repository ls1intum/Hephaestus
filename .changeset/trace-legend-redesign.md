The legend of a practice review's trace is easier to read. It opens at its top and groups the marks into stages, steps, where a path ends, observations and feedback, connections, the rail, and icons. Each mark has a short name and one line, and it looks the same as in the trace. The legend now shows every mark a stage can carry, the chips for not applicable, marked incorrect and the observation you started from, and the connection lines. Where a piece of feedback went now shows the tags the trace shows.
