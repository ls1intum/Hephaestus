The trace now gives one reason a practice could not be measured instead of three that contradict each
other. A source nothing captured was reported as not captured, captured only in part, and captured
empty all at once. It now says only that it was not captured; "captured only in part" and "captured
empty" appear only where a capture actually happened, which is what separates "we looked and there
was nothing there" from "we never got to look".
