Findings from a review of past work are no longer invisible. Confirming a campaign to review already-existing pull requests and issues used to spend real money and then show the results to nobody: every developer-facing read filtered them out, so a campaign's findings existed only in the database. They now flow through the same reads as live feedback — the reflective read model, the mentor's history of what it can refer to, and the prior-observations a later review is given — each carrying what occasioned it, so a surface can say an item came from a review of past work rather than passing it off as something that just happened.

They stay out of the per-practice trend, which answers "how is this changing over time" and would be distorted by a one-off sweep over months of history. Nothing is posted on the pull requests or issues themselves: a campaign reviews work that is already finished, and commenting on it would notify people about things they can no longer act on.

The admin findings list can now filter by what occasioned a measurement — live, requested by hand, or from a campaign — so a campaign's results can be judged on their own rather than blended into everything else.
