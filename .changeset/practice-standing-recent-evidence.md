Where you stand on a practice now follows your most recently reviewed work instead of the whole 90-day record. Two problem-free work items in a row are enough for a practice to read as going well again, so fixing a habit becomes visible within two reviews — and a problem on your newest work item registers just as quickly. How much of the work went well is counted rather than merely whether anything went wrong, so a single problem among otherwise clean reviews no longer weighs the same as a run of them. The list of feedback below the standing is unchanged: it stays the complete record of what the window raised, while the standing above it describes where things stand now.
