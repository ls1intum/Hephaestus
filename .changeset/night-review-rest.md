On the Practice profile, the folds name the window the standings are read from. They say "Every review in the last 90 days found ..." and "No review has observed ... in the last 90 days". The group fold ends "Open a group to see why", which also holds for a group with no practices. A row in the fold "Does not apply to your work yet" shows that badge, not "Nothing to report yet".

The empty "Resolved and closed" tab has new words. A card moves there once your work resolves it, you mark it yourself, or your workspace changes its practice.

- A screen reader now hears the sentence behind a standing badge, a trend and a feedback rating.
- On a phone, a tap opens what a review's status icon means.
- Closing the last panel returns the focus to what opened it.
- This also holds after a menu or a filter inside the panel was used.
- A panel closed by a slow action no longer closes or reopens another one.
- At phone width, the tables of Practices across the workspace stack their rows.
- At phone width, a long work label is cut with an ellipsis.
- At phone width, the rows of the Reviews, Observations and Feedback lists line up.
- At phone width, the figure tiles keep their height while they load.
- A full stop no longer wraps away from a work link.
- On a feedback card, "in" stays with the group name.
