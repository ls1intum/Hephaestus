Small fixes in **Practice reviews**.

- **Request review** on the sheet of a piece of work and **Request another review** on the sheet of a review ask you to confirm first, as the button in a row does. On the sheet of a piece of work, the button is disabled while a review of the work is queued or running.
- In the **Practices** table, a **Group** filter that no practice of the range belongs to no longer hides every row. The number of hidden turned-off practices counts only the practices that the **Group** filter shows.
- The practice summary above the **Observations** list follows the tracked practice, as the list does. When the list has no first day, the summary counts **All time**.
- An older link to the page of a review keeps the practice, the dates and the person that it carried.
- The sheet of a piece of work still shows the answer of the server when it opens again from the cache.
- The (i) texts of the **Queue** card and the breakdown card say "created", as the **Reviews** list does, and say which table each count opens.
