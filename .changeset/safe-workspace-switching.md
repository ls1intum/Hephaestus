Switching workspaces no longer carries a page that belongs to the workspace you are leaving — a conversation, a person or a practice opens the new workspace's home page instead, with a note explaining why, and a page option that names something in the workspace you are leaving, such as a team filter, is cleared while options that mean the same in any workspace, such as sorting and the time range, carry over. A link to a workspace you can no longer access opens a workspace you can. Hephaestus opens your first workspace when you sign in rather than the one you last used.
