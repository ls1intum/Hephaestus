The instance practice catalog now looks and works like the workspace one: each area
appears once with its practices inside it, the icon and color you pick for an area
shows up in the list, and turning an entry on or off is a switch instead of a pair of
buttons. A waiting Hephaestus update can now be declined as well as taken — the
"Keep our version" button previously did nothing, so the update count could never go
down.
