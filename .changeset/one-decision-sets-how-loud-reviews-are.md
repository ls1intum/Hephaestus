Turning practice reviews down is now one decision instead of one per practice. A workspace sets how much the system does on its own — Off, Observe (it reviews and records everything, and tells nobody) or Deliver — and every area and practice follows that until you say otherwise. An area can override the workspace, a practice can override its area, and anything you have not decided shows as inherited, with the level it came from and a way to reset it. The practice list also now summarises how many practices sit at each setting, per area and overall, so you can see what a workspace is actually doing without opening a hundred rows.

Where feedback goes is now a separate workspace setting — the mentor conversation only, or also on the work itself — instead of being mixed into the per-practice setting. Reviews are unchanged unless you change something: the old Off and Measure carry over (Measure is now called Observe), and Engage becomes the workspace default, so nothing gets louder or quieter on upgrade.

**Operators:** practices that were on Coach become Observe. Coach meant "speak in the mentor conversation but never on the work", which mixed how much with where, and where is now a workspace setting — so it could not be carried over exactly and was resolved to the quieter option deliberately. To get the old behaviour back, set the workspace to *In the mentor conversation only* and raise those practices to Deliver. If you never used Coach, there is nothing to do.
