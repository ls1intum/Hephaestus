**Trace** on any row in Practice reviews opens the story of the record in a wide sheet: what started the review, the review, its observations, the feedback written from them, where each piece went, and what became of it.

- One sentence on top says what became of the record, for example "This feedback did not reach the developer: the work was merged 7 min before it was written." It comes from the record itself, not from a model. A review that assessed nothing, because its evidence could not be read or an earlier review on the same code had answered every practice, says so.
- A line under it names the bottleneck: a failed review with its error, a canceled one with its reason, a hold, feedback withheld with its reason in plain words, or the longest wait. Its tone says what the loss asks of an admin: red for a failed delivery, amber for feedback lost to timing, grey for feedback the review, the developer or an admin held back, and a grey "No bottleneck" when nothing stopped. Feedback withheld because the work was already merged says when the merge fell: before the review was asked for, in the queue, or while it ran.
- A strip shows where the time went: until the review was asked for, the queue, preparation and execution (or one run for older reviews), until the feedback reached the developer, and until it was first read. A wait of 30 minutes or more is coloured in the trace. The overview marks the same wait with an icon and words.
- The story reads in time order in four stages, Trigger, Review, Feedback and Effect, each with its own icon and header, and marks the stage where the path stopped. The record you opened the trace from is marked **You started here**, and the mark stays while you open other records over the trace. The story ends where the record's path ended: it worked, it did not arrive and why, or it is still open.
- The observations and the feedback of a stage are tables. Each feedback row has its letter, its channel as a tag, its state, the observations it was written from by number, and its time. Each observation row has its number, practice, outcome, severity, the feedback written from it by letter, and its time, and says when the developer disputes its feedback. The observation table opens and closes on its own button and with **Expand all**. A review's observations keep the same numbers in every trace of that review. On a narrow screen, the rows stack.
- **Show** turns on times, system info, siblings and connections for the whole trace, and your browser keeps your choice. With **System info**, the facts show under the record you started from. With **Connections**, each line runs from the middle of an observation's card to the middle of each piece of feedback written from it, follows the cards when words wrap, a part opens or the sheet changes width, and the line that spans more runs outside the shorter ones. **Expand all** opens every folded part and shows only what a step's line does not already say; **Collapse all** folds them again. **Legend** explains each mark.
- Filter the trace to the feedback, to what was lost or to one practice. A step opens its record's sheet over the trace; Escape closes the sheet first.
- The review says which of the work's reviews it is, when its results failed or wait to be processed, and when it was requested or backfilled. A prepared piece of feedback says where it goes: posted on the work, shown on the developer's practice pages, or raised in their next conversation with Heph. A card on the practice pages that nobody opened yet says so.
- For each piece of feedback the trace says whether the developer answered, whether they dispute it, with the reason they wrote to the admins, and whether their later work resolved it. How they rated it, what else they decided and any note beside it stay private to them.
- A merged pull request whose merge time is missing takes it from the review or the merge moment, and feedback delivered in part with the rest withheld reads as delivered in part. The trace of work that no review has started on says that the work is queued or waiting, and why.
- An older link to the page of a review that pointed to one observation or piece of feedback opens the trace of that record.
