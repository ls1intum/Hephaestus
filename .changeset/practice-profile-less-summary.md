The Practice profile shows less above and beside your feedback:

- Heph's summary above your feedback is gone: the block with what is holding up well, what needs your attention, what changed and the reviewed work. Your feedback cards come first under the header.
- A practice group no longer shows Heph's card above its tabs, with what is holding up well, the next step and the reviewed work. The group opens on its practices.
- "About this practice" and "About this group" no longer show "Where you stand". The standing and the trend stay in the header; the tab keeps the words on the practice or the group.
- The practice group table and a group's practice list no longer repeat "There is new feedback, seen on ..." for a practice. The feedback card says it, so the row shows the other changes instead.
