Practice reviews in workspace administration is now four tabs, Overview, Reviews, Observations and Feedback, with one way to each record.

- The developer, group, practice and date filters sit above the tabs, marked "Applies to every tab", and stay as you switch tabs. Each tab shows its own filters in one row that wraps, with the sort beside them, and the applied filters show as pills. The Reviews, Observations and Feedback tabs show how many records they hold under the shared filters, for example **Reviews 1,688**.
- Reviews, Observations and Feedback are tables grouped by day. A click anywhere on a row opens the record's sheet. Each row has **Track**, which narrows every tab to the related records, and **Trace**, which opens the record's story, in its **Actions** column. A later review of the same work reads "Review 2 of 3", a review someone asked for is tagged **Requested**, as it is on the developer's Practice profile, and a chip beside the work marks a review that needs a look: on hold, failed, timed out, replaced by a newer one, failed to process its results, or finished after the merge when the merge did not start it. The time column of the Reviews tab and its date filter say **Created**.
- A row of the Reviews tab, and a row of the work no review started on, also has **Request review** in that column. It asks for a new review of that work, as **Request another review** on a review's sheet does, after you confirm, because the review runs as an agent job. It is disabled, with a tooltip that says why, while a review of the same work is queued or running and for work nobody can request a review for.
- The work on which no review started, and why, is the Reviews tab with Status "No review started".
- The lists load more rows as you scroll. "Show more" stays at the end for keyboard use.
- The Practice filter and the overview's Practices table leave out practices turned off today; "Show turned-off practices" shows them. The Practices table has a Group filter, and its rows open the practice's sheet.
- Where a piece of feedback went is its **Channel**, in the filter, the Feedback tab's column, the sheet and the trace, each with the same icon everywhere. The **Channel** filter offers the locations: **On GitHub**, **On GitLab**, **With Heph in the app**, **With Heph in Slack** or **On their practice pages**. **On the work** and **In conversation** cover feedback with no known provider. A developer's feedback can be counted by delivery and by place.
- The overview can show **Today**, compared with yesterday.
- Older links to a review's page, the Work tab or a record panel open the matching trace, sheet or list. **Review details** in the browser extension opens the reviews of that work with its sheet open.
