The tables in **Practice reviews** are easier to scan and to act on.

- **Track**, **Trace** and **Request review** sit in their own **Actions** column as full-size buttons, and the (i) in its header says what each does. A click in that column never opens the row.
- The **Request review** confirmation is a real modal: the page behind it does not respond, Escape closes it, and the focus returns to the button.
- **Reviews** drops the **Result** column. A chip beside the work marks a review that is on hold, failed, timed out, replaced by a newer one, failed to process its results, or finished after the merge when the merge did not start it.
- The **Status** filter on **Reviews** offers **On hold**.
- **Feedback** drops the text preview. **Outcome** shows its icon and label, and a withheld row says why in the words of the **Why withheld** filter. Each row is named by its channel, recipient and work.
- The sheet of work in a repository the workspace does not monitor shows what the server answered.
