The mentor now composes a coaching turn from structured notes and the live conversation instead of reading
out a question written hours earlier. A review hands the mentor the situation, coaching goal, evidence,
and an observable success signal. The mentor can ask, explain, or wait based on the conversation rather
than following a fixed ask-first script, and it can inspect the authorized observation evidence rather than
trusting a lossy summary.

Prepared coaching uses one strict schema; malformed or oversized notes are rejected rather than truncated
or interpreted through a retired compatibility shape.
