Practices about written documentation now actually run. A workspace with Outline connected has been
able to bind a practice to "a document was published" or "its content changed" and see it listed as
live — but nothing ever started a review, so the practice was silent and indistinguishable from one
that simply had nothing to say. Publishing or editing a mirrored wiki document now starts a review of
that document and records the results against its author, so the bundled "Say what else you
considered" practice reports on decision records for the first time.

A document review reads only the document — its title, collection, authorship and body — and lands on
the author's own profile. Nothing is posted back into the wiki.

Where a review still does not start, the reason is recorded rather than lost: the document's author
has not linked their account, every bound practice is turned off, the workspace's budget is spent.
The ones an administrator can lift are retried automatically once they do.
