The Review page now says what each practice is. Every row names the kind of work it reviews and why the practice exists, so deciding how much Hephaestus may do on its own no longer means recognising a practice by its name alone. The tier controls for areas and practices line up in one column instead of starting at a different place on every row, and on a phone the Off/Propose/Deliver choices are readable again — they were being squeezed down to a single letter each.
